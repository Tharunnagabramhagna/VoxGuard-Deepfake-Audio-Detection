# Historical Results Archive: Initial Audit Baseline

## 1. Provenance Information
- **Archive Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Originating Code:** `scripts/evaluate_models.py` (Revision prior to Phase 2 bug fix)
- **Dataset Partition Used:** Synthetic fixture manifests `asvspoof_{train,dev,eval}_mini.csv` ($N=30$ per partition: 10 bonafide, 20 spoof)
- **Audio Nature:** Synthetic sine tones and additive white Gaussian noise generated by `src/data/test_fixtures.py` (Not real speech).

## 2. Known Artifact Flaws & Anomalies
1. **Noise Robustness Label Corruption:** The AWGN results in `evaluation_summary.json` (showing 100% accuracy under 0 dB noise) were caused by a dictionary key mismatch where `audio_stem_to_label.get(f.stem, 0)` assigned 0 (bonafide) to all 30 noise evaluation samples.
2. **Underfitting on Small Fixture Subset:** The 30-sample training size caused severe underfitting and logit inversion (ROC-AUC 0.11–0.28).
3. **External CD-ADD Data:** Evaluated on 50 procedural synthetic audio files from `create_cd_add_test_fixtures()`, not the real Hugging Face CD-ADD corpus.
